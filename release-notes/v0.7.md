Billy is a Gemini assistant for Pebble watches. Ask by voice; Billy answers with short text and Pebble-style cards, and with the Android companion it can act on your phone and Google account.

## Install
- **Watch app (iPhone or Android):** open `Billy-0.7.pbw` on your phone and install it with the Pebble app.
- **Gemini key (free):** get one at https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey and paste it in Billy's settings in the Pebble app, or in Billy Companion if you use it.
- **Billy Companion (Android, optional):** install `BillyCompanion-0.7.apk` and follow the steps on its screen. It installs over any earlier version, including 0.5, and keeps your setup.

## What's new in 0.7
- **Fixed:** every answer failing with "'google_maps' and 'code execution' cannot be combined", after a change on Google's side.
- **Models:** the list matches what Google offers today (3.8 Flash, 3.6 Flash, 3.5 Flash-Lite, 3.1 Flash-Lite, 3.1 Pro). If you had 3.7 Flash or 3.5 Flash, Billy moves you to 3.8 Flash. Pick the model and how hard it thinks on the watch under **Settings**; that choice now always wins.
- **Free keys:** when Google's free allowance runs out, Billy says which limit you hit and what to do. 3.5 Flash-Lite has a much bigger free daily allowance than 3.8 Flash.
- **No silent fallbacks:** if something fails, Billy tells you instead of quietly answering with fewer features. Billy Companion shows a notification that opens a ready-made bug report (timings only, never your questions or answers).
- **Faster and steadier:** Billy Companion keeps the phone awake while it answers; turn on "Stay connected when the screen is off" in its step 2. Personal Google Photos load faster.
- **Fewer pictures:** pictures appear when you ask to see something, and the text comes first.
- **Update reminders:** the watch app reminds you (once a day) if your Billy Companion is too old, and Billy Companion now tells you itself when a new version is on GitHub.
- **Typing to the watch:** a prompt sent from Billy Companion's test box continues the conversation that's open.
- **Pebble plugins (preview):** with plugins turned on in the Pebble app (Settings, Plugins), Billy can read the phone's own calendar (iPhone too), control music, ring your phone, open watch apps and use installed plugins. Off unless you turn plugins on.

## Notes
- Google Home: Billy can't switch smart-home devices. Google doesn't allow Google Home control from the Gemini website that Billy's Gemini account link uses, so Billy now says so (and offers to open the Google Home app) instead of claiming it worked.
- You pay Google for any Gemini usage beyond the free tier; Billy has no server.
