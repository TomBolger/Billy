Billy is a Gemini assistant for Pebble watches. This is a small update to the watch app only.

## Install
- **Watch app:** update Billy from the Pebble app store, or open `Billy-0.7.1.pbw` on your phone and install it with the Pebble app.
- **Billy Companion:** no update needed. Keep using 0.7 (download it from the [0.7 release](https://github.com/TomBolger/Billy/releases/tag/v0.7) if you don't have it yet).

## What's new in 0.7.1
- **Round watches (Pebble Time Round, Pebble Round 2):** the goat on the home screen fits the circle, and his speech bubble keeps its text inside.
- **Round watches:** "thinking" bars, like "Searching your Google Photos", stretch edge to edge.
- **Pebble Round 2:** the About art fills the screen and no longer scrolls too far.
- **Colour watches:** cleaned up the white patch around the goat's beard on the About screen.
