Billy is a Gemini assistant for Pebble watches. Ask by voice; Billy answers with short text and Pebble-style cards, and with the Android companion it can act on your phone and Google account.

## Install
- **Watch app (iPhone or Android):** open `Billy-0.6.pbw` on your phone and install it with the Pebble app.
- **Gemini key (free):** get one at https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey and paste it in Billy's settings in the Pebble app, or in Billy Companion if you use it.
- **Billy Companion (Android, optional):** install `BillyCompanion-0.6.apk` and follow the steps on its screen. Only the Gemini key is required.

## What's new in 0.6
- **Set up Google yourself:** Billy Companion walks you through connecting Calendar, Tasks, Gmail, Drive and Contacts using your own free Google project, with a button for each step. No invitation needed. Google will warn that the app isn't verified, because it's your own project: tap Advanced, then Go to Billy (unsafe).
- **Clearer first-time setup** in Billy Companion and in the watch app's settings.

## Upgrading from 0.5 or earlier
Billy Companion has a new signing key, so Android won't install 0.6 over an older copy. Uninstall the old Billy Companion first, then install this one and set it up again.

## Notes
- You pay Google for any Gemini usage beyond the free tier; Billy has no server.
