# Billy 0.5
## More watches
Billy now runs on Pebble Time, Time Round, Pebble 2, Pebble 2 Duo and Pebble Round 2, as well as Pebble Time 2.
## Your Gemini account
With Billy Companion you can link your own Gemini account to find photos in your Google Photos library and use what Gemini knows about you.
## More reliable
Answers no longer get lost or land in the wrong chat, reminders only say "set" once they really are, and you can read a whole message before sending it.
