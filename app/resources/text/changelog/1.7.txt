# Billy 0.7
## Model and thinking on the watch
Pick the Gemini model and how hard it thinks under Settings, right on the watch. The model list matches what Google offers today.
## Clearer problems
When something goes wrong, Billy says what happened instead of quietly answering with fewer features, and you can report it from the menu.
## Fewer pictures
Pictures show up when you ask to see something, not on every answer.
## Update Billy Companion
On Android? Install Billy Companion 0.7 from github.com/TomBolger/Billy/releases. It installs over the old one and keeps your setup. From now on it tells you when there's a new version.
